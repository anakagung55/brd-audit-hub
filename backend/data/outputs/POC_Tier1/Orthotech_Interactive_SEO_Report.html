Critical & Important Findings

High & Medium Severity

Four findings requiring attention to improve search visibility and AI extraction.

Website Health
Core technical SEO elements
2 Severity
The website appears to have fundamental technical SEO elements in place, supporting basic crawlability and indexability.
Commercial ConsequenceA solid technical foundation ensures search engines can efficiently access, crawl, and index the website's content, which is crucial for organic visibility.
→Continue regular monitoring of core web vitals, mobile-friendliness, and site speed. Implement a robust internal linking strategy to distribute link equity effectively.
🔧 Light lift Priority 1
Google Listing
H1 tag relevance to page content
0 Severity
The H1 tag 'ABOUTORTHOTECH LABORATORY & ORTHOTICS' does not accurately reflect the primary content of the page, which is a testimonial. This creates a disconnect for users and search engines.
Commercial ConsequenceSearch engines may struggle to understand the primary topic of the page, potentially leading to lower rankings for relevant testimonial-related queries. Users might also be confused if the heading doesn't match the content.
→Update the H1 tag to accurately describe the page's content, for example, 'Client testimonials' or 'What our clients say'.
🔧 Light lift Priority 2
Business Identity
General JSON-LD schema implementation
2 Severity
General JSON-LD schema markup is present on the page.
Commercial ConsequenceStructured data helps search engines understand the content and context of the page, potentially enhancing visibility in search results through rich snippets.
→Regularly review and update existing schema to ensure it accurately reflects current content and adheres to the latest schema.org guidelines.
🔧 Light lift Priority 3
AI Readability
Content structure for answer engine optimisation
2 Severity
The content is presented as a testimonial, which is a narrative format. While clear, it's not inherently structured for direct Q&A extraction by answer engines.
Commercial ConsequenceWhile testimonials are valuable for social proof, their narrative format may not directly contribute to answer engine visibility for specific factual queries.
→Consider adding a dedicated FAQ section on relevant service pages to directly address common customer questions, optimising for answer engine visibility.
🛠️ Moderate build Priority 4

Customer Questions
Absence of FAQ content and schema
1 Severity
No visual FAQ content or corresponding FAQPage schema markup was detected on the page. This represents a missed opportunity to capture answer engine visibility.
Commercial ConsequenceWithout FAQ content and schema, the website misses opportunities to appear in rich results for common questions, potentially reducing organic traffic and visibility in voice search.
→Research common questions related to orthotics and Orthotech's services. Create a dedicated FAQ section on relevant pages (e.g., service pages or a general FAQ page) and implement FAQPage JSON-LD schema markup to enhance answer engine visibility.
🛠️ Moderate build
AI Presence
Optimisation for AI answer engines
1 Severity
While general JSON-LD schema is present, the absence of specific FAQ content and corresponding schema limits the page's ability to generate direct answers in AI search results.
Commercial ConsequenceThe website may not be fully optimised to provide direct answers to user queries via AI answer engines, potentially losing out on valuable 'position zero' visibility and traffic.
→Develop and integrate structured Q&A content (FAQs) with appropriate schema markup on relevant pages. This will provide clear, concise answers that AI answer engines can easily extract and present to users.
🛠️ Moderate build
