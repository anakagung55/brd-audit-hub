Critical & Important Findings

High & Medium Severity

Four findings requiring attention to improve search visibility and AI extraction.

Website Health
Core technical SEO foundations are sound
Good Severity
The website appears to have a solid technical foundation, indicating good crawlability and indexability for search engines.
Commercial ConsequenceA strong technical foundation ensures that search engines can efficiently discover, crawl, and index the website's content, which is crucial for organic visibility and ranking.
→Continue to monitor core web vitals and other technical SEO metrics to maintain optimal performance and user experience.
🔧 Light lift Priority 1
Google Listing
Optimise meta title and H1 for consistency and conciseness
Medium Severity
The H1 tag is present and relevant, but there's a minor inconsistency in keyword usage ('scrapmetal' vs. 'scrap metal'). The meta title could also be more concise while retaining key service offerings.
Commercial ConsequenceInconsistent keyword usage can slightly dilute keyword relevance signals. A less optimised meta title might reduce click-through rates from search results, impacting organic traffic.
→Refine the H1 for conciseness and ensure consistent use of 'scrap metal' (two words) across all on-page elements. Optimise the meta title to be more impactful and keyword-rich, encouraging higher click-through rates.
⚡ Quick win Priority 2
Business Identity
Foundational JSON-LD schema markup is present
Good Severity
General JSON-LD schema markup is present on the page, indicating a foundational level of structured data implementation.
Commercial ConsequenceThe presence of structured data helps search engines understand the context and entities on the page, which can improve how the website is represented in search results and contribute to better visibility.
→Conduct a comprehensive schema audit to ensure all relevant entity types (e.g., LocalBusiness, Service, Product) are appropriately marked up to provide maximum context to search engines.
🛠️ Moderate build Priority 3
AI Readability
Content is well-structured for user and AI comprehension
Good Severity
The page content is well-structured with clear headings and supporting information, making it easy for users and AI to understand the core offering and value proposition.
Commercial ConsequenceClear and well-organised content improves user engagement and helps AI answer engines extract information accurately, leading to better visibility in AEO results and a positive user experience.
→Continue to develop content that directly answers potential user questions and provides clear value propositions, utilising semantic HTML where appropriate to further enhance structure.
🔧 Light lift Priority 4

Customer Questions
Missing FAQPage schema for existing FAQ content
High Severity
FAQ content exists on the /commercial-and-industrial-services page but lacks FAQPage schema markup. Without this structured data, AI answer engines cannot reliably identify it as Q&A content.
Commercial ConsequenceFailing to implement FAQPage schema means the website misses out on opportunities for rich results in search (e.g., expandable snippets) and direct answers in AI-powered search, reducing visibility and click potential.
→Inject FAQPage JSON-LD into the of the page. No new section needed - schema-only fix.
⚡ Quick win
AI Presence
Improve AI visibility through comprehensive schema implementation
Medium Severity
While some JSON-LD schema is present, the absence of specific schema types like FAQPage for existing visual FAQs limits the site's ability to appear in AI-powered answer engines for direct questions.
Commercial ConsequenceLimited AI visibility means the website may not be featured in 'answer boxes' or direct responses from AI search, leading to missed opportunities for top-of-funnel exposure and authority building.
→Implement FAQPage schema for all visually presented FAQs to enhance visibility in AI answer engines and rich results. Additionally, explore other relevant schema types to provide comprehensive signals to AI.
🛠️ Moderate build
